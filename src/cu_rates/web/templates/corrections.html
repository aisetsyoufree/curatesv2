Report a Correction or Problem

Before you write

If something on this site looks wrong, tell us. We check every credible report against the credit union's own published information, and we may remove a value while we check.

Please do not send applications, account numbers, Social Security numbers, financial records, or any other personal data. We do not need them and we do not want them. If personal data arrives anyway, we remove it from our working records.

  • What we do with it: your report is stored as a case. We aim to look at a credible report about a rate, a wrong credit union or product, privacy, or security within one business day, and other reports within five business days. These are targets, not promises, and we are not a lender: we cannot change your loan, quote you a rate, or contact a credit union for you.
  • Corrections need a source: we change a published value only when the credit union's own published information supports it. A report on its own is never treated as a source.
  • Your reply address is optional. If you give one, it is used only to reply to this report, is kept apart from the case record, is never sent to a credit union or vendor without your permission, and is deleted 90 days after the case is resolved. We do not use it for marketing.
