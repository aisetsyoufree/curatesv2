Methodology and Comparison Principles

Provenance and Verification Architecture

CU-Rates.org is designed around a deterministic, evidence-backed data architecture. Once data collection is live, automated routines and deterministic parsers are designed to extract public lending terms from approved sources without altering their meaning.

  • Immutable Storage: Raw responses (HTML, documents, and network responses) will be archived in content-addressed storage before any parsing occurs.
  • Field-Level Traceability: Once data is published, every data point will retain its exact locator path, text hash, collection timestamp, and parser version.
  • Human-Approved Sources: Third-party sources and discovered URLs will be treated as untrusted until verified by human reviewers.

Loan Taxonomy and Financial Rigor

Financial terminology in home lending requires precision to avoid misleading comparisons:

  • Product Family Separation: First mortgages, closed-end home equity loans, and open-end lines of credit are modeled as distinct financial structures.
  • Purpose Distinction: Refinancing is modeled as a loan purpose, not an independent product family. Purchase loans and refinancing programs are never conflated.
  • Interest Rates and APRs: The initial interest rate and the Annual Percentage Rate represent different financial measurements and are never substituted for one another.
  • Variable and Introductory Terms: Introductory teaser rates on credit lines are designed to be tracked separately from ongoing index-and-margin variable structures.

Bounded Freshness and Emergency Suppression

Advertised loan offerings change frequently. Once data publication is live, the platform is designed to prevent outdated data from remaining visible through strict planned controls:

  • Planned Freshness Expiry: Once collection is live, observations will undergo scheduled daily re-observation. The platform is designed so that unconfirmed observations will degrade after 72 hours, and comparison rankings will be withdrawn after 96 hours if timely updates are not received.
  • Planned Emergency Suppression: An independent denylist mechanism is designed to allow immediate suppression of any offer, product, or institution record upon reviewer action.
  • No Runtime Artificial Intelligence: Routine collection, extraction, normalization, and publication are designed to run deterministically without runtime language models.
