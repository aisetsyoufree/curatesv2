Methodology and Comparison Principles

Provenance and Verification Architecture

CU-Rates.org operates under a deterministic, evidence-backed data architecture. Automated routines and deterministic parsers extract published lending terms from approved sources without altering their meaning.

  • Immutable Storage: Raw responses (HTML, documents, and network responses) are archived in a content-addressed storage repository before any parsing occurs.
  • Field-Level Traceability: Every published data point retains its exact locator path, text hash, collection timestamp, and parser version.
  • Human-Approved Sources: Third-party sources and discovered URLs are treated as untrusted until verified by human reviewers.

Loan Taxonomy and Financial Rigor

Financial terminology in home lending requires precision to avoid misleading comparisons:

  • Product Family Separation: First mortgages, closed-end home equity loans, and open-end lines of credit are modeled as distinct financial structures.
  • Purpose Distinction: Refinancing is modeled as a loan purpose, not an independent product family. Purchase loans and refinancing programs are never conflated.
  • Interest Rates and APRs: The initial interest rate and the Annual Percentage Rate represent different financial measurements and are never substituted for one another.
  • Variable and Introductory Terms: Introductory teaser rates on credit lines are tracked separately from ongoing index-and-margin variable structures.

Bounded Freshness and Emergency Suppression

Advertised loan offerings change frequently. To prevent outdated data from remaining visible:

  • Automated Freshness Expiry: Observations undergo scheduled re-observation. Unconfirmed observations degrade and comparison rankings are automatically withdrawn if timely updates are not received.
  • Emergency Suppression: An independent denylist mechanism allows immediate suppression of any offer, product, or institution record upon review.
  • No Runtime Artificial Intelligence: Routine collection, extraction, normalization, and publication run deterministically without runtime language models.
