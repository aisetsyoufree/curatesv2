Massachusetts Credit Union Residential Lending Directory

About This Directory

CU-Rates.org is an educational, evidence-backed public directory accounting for Massachusetts credit unions that offer residential home-lending products. The project helps consumers discover and evaluate publicly advertised offers through objective, standardized criteria.

The directory distinguishes residential loan products into clear families—first mortgages, fixed-term home equity loans, and home equity lines of credit—without conflating purchase loans with refinancing purposes or promotional introductory periods.

Core Operating Principles

Once data publication is live, every observation in this directory will be governed by strict, non-negotiable data integrity rules:

  • Fail-Closed Publication: The pipeline is designed so that data with uncertain provenance, ambiguity, or unresolved verification will be quarantined and never published.
  • Evidence-Backed Provenance: Every published entry will trace to an immutable, timestamped raw artifact locator from an approved public source.
  • Standardized Scenarios: Comparisons will be evaluated strictly against identical canonical borrower scenarios to maintain fair, like-for-like transparency.
  • Explicit Product States: Institutions that do not publish rates, require authentication, or provide quotes by inquiry only will be cataloged with their verified status rather than ungrounded assumptions.

Read our full Methodology and Comparison Principles to learn more about our verification pipeline and standards.

Current System Status

The collection and verification pipeline is currently completing vertical-slice testing and institution roster reconciliation. Verified comparison data will appear here once baseline data snapshots pass automated integrity and governance gates.
