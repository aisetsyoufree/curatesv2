Privacy Policy and Consent Notice

Our Privacy-First Commitment

CU-Rates.org is designed from the ground up to respect consumer privacy. We operate as an independent educational resource and do not collect, monetize, or broker personal borrower data.

  • No Borrower Lead Generation: We do not ask for personal identification numbers, Social Security Numbers, phone numbers, or physical street addresses, and the only email address we ever accept is the optional reply address you may give when you report a correction (see below). We do not sell user inquiries to lenders, brokers, or lead aggregators.
  • No Personalized Pricing Forms: Once comparison data is published, all information presented in the directory will reflect publicly advertised terms collected from institutional sources, not individualized quotes based on user submissions.

Analytics and Measurement

We believe in transparent, consent-driven measurement:

  • Disabled by Default: Analytics scripts and measurement tags are completely disabled by default. No third-party network requests or tracking scripts are loaded before you explicitly allow analytics.
  • Your Choice: When optional analytics is configured, Google Consent Mode v2 starts with analytics and advertising storage denied. Only choosing “Allow analytics” loads Google Analytics and permits its cookies. Choosing “Decline or withdraw analytics” stops analytics on subsequent pages and removes this site’s Google Analytics cookies. Advertising consent stays denied. The controls below work without JavaScript; analytics needs JavaScript to run.
  • Preference Storage: Only after you choose, a secure first-party preference cookie saves your allow or decline decision for 180 days. It is separate from Google Analytics cookies. The site sends page paths to analytics after consent, excluding query strings and referrers.
  • No Third-Party Advertising: The website carries no third-party banner advertisements, advertising trackers, or promotional remarketing pixels.

Reporting a Correction

If you report a problem through our correction form, we store what you type (the page, the value, your reason and any web address you give) as a case so that we can check it and keep an honest record of what we decided. We ask you not to include personal or account details; if you do, we remove them from our working records.

  • Reply address (optional): kept separately from the case, used only to reply to you, never sent to a credit union or vendor without your permission, and deleted 90 days after the case is resolved.
  • No account, no cookies: the form sets no cookies and needs no sign-in. We do not use reports for marketing or lead generation.

Server Logs and Technical Data

Like standard web services, our web server may log basic technical network metadata (such as IP address, user agent, requested path, and response status) strictly for security monitoring, denial-of-service defense, and service reliability. These operational logs are retained temporarily and are never combined with personal borrower profiles.

If you have questions about our privacy practices or educational mission, please consult our Methodology page.
