Privacy Policy and Consent Notice

Our Privacy-First Commitment

CU-Rates.org is designed from the ground up to respect consumer privacy. We operate as an independent educational resource and do not collect, monetize, or broker personal borrower data.

  • No Borrower Lead Generation: We do not ask for personal identification numbers, Social Security Numbers, phone numbers, email addresses, or physical street addresses. We do not sell user inquiries to lenders, brokers, or lead aggregators.
  • No Personalized Pricing Forms: Once comparison data is published, all information presented in the directory will reflect publicly advertised terms collected from institutional sources, not individualized quotes based on user submissions.

Analytics and Measurement

We believe in transparent, consent-driven measurement:

  • Disabled by Default: Analytics scripts and measurement tags are completely disabled by default. No third-party network requests or tracking scripts are loaded when you visit this website.
  • Consent Mode Integration: If analytics are configured in the future, the platform implements Google Consent Mode with all storage parameters set to denied unless explicit consent is provided by the user.
  • No Third-Party Advertising: The website carries no third-party banner advertisements, advertising trackers, or promotional remarketing pixels.

Server Logs and Technical Data

Like standard web services, our web server may log basic technical network metadata (such as IP address, user agent, requested path, and response status) strictly for security monitoring, denial-of-service defense, and service reliability. These operational logs are retained temporarily and are never combined with personal borrower profiles.

If you have questions about our privacy practices or educational mission, please consult our Methodology page.
