How to read coverage

Verified means a verified public offer was observed within the last 72 hours. Stale means verified offers exist but none was observed within 72 hours. Unknown means this site has no verified public information for the product family. It does not mean the product is not offered, that no rate is published, or that collection is blocked or unsupported: those states cannot yet be derived from the public data and are never guessed.

Nothing unverified is presented as current fact on this site. Each figure shown carries the time it was observed, and offers that are withdrawn, expired, or suppressed disappear. See the methodology. If something looks wrong, report a correction or problem.
