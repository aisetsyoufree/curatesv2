# T007 — Collection, evidence, and licensing policy

## Objective

Turn the project's collection ethics, public-evidence boundaries, corrections, and
licensing decisions into an implementable policy approved by Sid.

## Dependencies

T004 is done. Sid must approve before T032 begins.

## Non-goals

No source scraping, legal conclusion, or automated robots/ToS bypass decision.

## Allowed changes

Policy documents, license files, and this task packet. The integrator records final
decisions in `PROJECT.md`.

## Acceptance criteria

- Define contact User-Agent, per-host rate/concurrency defaults, caching, retries,
  backoff, robots decisions, ToS escalation, authentication/CAPTCHA prohibition,
  screenshots, form inputs, retention, and source-abandonment review.
- Permit form interaction only for anonymous public calculators approved per source;
  prefer ZIP-only geography and never submit a real person's address, name, email,
  phone, or other lead-generating identifiers.
- Define public/private evidence fields, excerpt limits, correction intake, emergency
  withdrawal, and data-subject or lender contact handling.
- Add MIT for original code and CC BY 4.0 only for original docs and exported
  normalized datasets; third-party artifacts are excluded from relicensing.
- Include a source-approval checklist and explicit blocked/quote-only outcomes.
- Define trademark and logo-use boundaries and flag unresolved legal questions for
  counsel rather than presenting the policy as legal advice.

## Data, migration, and publication impact

No rate data changes. This policy becomes a required gate for source approval,
collection, evidence publication, corrections, and licensing.

## Required verification

- Cross-check every policy rule against Sections 5, 8, and D-06.
- Search for contradictory collection or licensing claims across tracked docs.
- `rg -n "bypass|CAPTCHA|personal|excerpt|retention|CC BY|MIT|logo" PROJECT.md policies tasks`
- Record Sid's approval and unresolved counsel questions in the handoff.
