## Findings

### Before T004

1. A worktree outside iCloud would still keep Git metadata in the iCloud repository.
   Define a full authoritative clone outside iCloud, freeze or retire the iCloud copy,
   and name the integration location.
2. T004 promised one verification command but listed four; it also omitted SQLite
   sidecar and coverage ignore rules, an explicit Python pin/mypy policy, and the
   impact section required by the task template.

### Important contract and sequencing issues

- Jumbo was described as ranked but had no jumbo scenario.
- Home-equity and HELOC scenarios lacked property value, first-lien balance, and
  credit score.
- Program taxonomy mixed conforming status, government guaranty, borrower program,
  and partner program.
- Shared CUSO/vendor URLs need explicit relationship semantics; a shared URL is not
  automatically a registry error.
- Workers were told both not to edit `PROJECT.md` and to resolve conflicts there.
- Roster and identity work could start before the contract/evidence system.
- Freshness and emergency withdrawal depended on the publisher being alive. Opus
  recommended per-row `valid_until`, read-time suppression, and an independent kill
  switch.
- A published SQLite file should not be swapped with live WAL sidecars; use immutable
  versioned snapshots and reopen on pointer change.
- Scenario matching needs `matches`, `conflicts`, and `unstated` semantics.
- Rendered DOM and image/OCR evidence need locator types; corrections need an entity.
- Anonymous form collection must not generate leads or submit a real person's data.
- T007 should address excerpt, logo, retention, and unresolved counsel questions.

### Agreements

Keep explicit-absence coverage, fail-closed publication, source-grounded evidence,
Decimal financial values, separate rate/APR/points semantics, post-intro HELOC ranking,
constrained auto-publication, bounded freshness, server-rendered Flask, private raw
artifacts, an atomic public database, polite collection, no runtime LLM, no launch ads,
and integrator-owned governance.

### Defer or reject

Defer more mortgage scenarios, government-program rankings, fee modeling, a certified
CMP, backup-tool selection, and out-of-state CUs. Do not add another planning cycle,
an architecture review of lint tooling, a business-day freshness calendar, or a
purge mechanism for immutable artifacts now.
